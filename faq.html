What does Trinity 718 actually do?

We provide fractional CFO support for founder-led brands in fitness, wellness, franchise, and beauty/CPG. This means: weekly KPI tracking, multi-unit financial visibility, cash flow planning, unit economics clarity, lender/investor reporting, and strategic finance coaching. We don't replace your bookkeeper—we sit above them and drive decision-grade financial clarity.

Do you replace our bookkeeper or accountant?

No. We work alongside your existing team. Your bookkeeper/accountant handles transactions and tax compliance; we focus on strategy, forecasting, and decision support. In fact, we often improve their workflow and give them direction on what to prioritize. Think CFO-level thinking + execution discipline + your team handling day-to-day.

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients feel a shift within 30 days: cleaner reporting, owned KPIs, and more confident decisions. By 60-90 days, you'll have mature dashboards, clear accountability, and tangible improvements in cash visibility or margin discipline. By 6 months, the finance infrastructure is self-sustaining and culturally embedded.

Is this only for founder-led companies?

Yes, we focus specifically on founder-led operators. We don't work well with highly federated teams or organizations where finance decisions are siloed. Our strength is working directly with founders who want to drive financial clarity and operational accountability.

Can we change our package as we grow?

Absolutely. Engagements expand or contract as your business needs evolve. Most clients start at Foundation or Operator and scale up as complexity or capital events increase. Others dial back if priorities shift. We design flexibility into every engagement.

What if our books are a mess?

That's exactly when you need us. A messy close process is our starting point, not a blocker. We help you build the discipline and systems to make future closes clean, fast, and reliable. Many clients come to us after a cleanup project, and we prevent them from sliding backward.

How do we know if we're ready for a fractional CFO?

You're ready if: revenue is $3M+, you have multiple locations or complex channels, founder is still operationally involved, and you know finance discipline would move the needle. You don't need to be VC-backed or PE-owned; bootstrapped and hybrid-funded companies love working with us because we understand their builder mentality.

What's the typical commitment?

We recommend a minimum 6-month engagement to build real systems and culture change. Most clients stay 12-24 months because the benefits compound. We're month-to-month after an initial term, but jumping in and out too quickly defeats the purpose of building disciplined finance infrastructure.
